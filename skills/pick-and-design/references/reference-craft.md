# Reference craft and implementation specifications

Use for turning a chosen direction into an implementation-ready specification, extracting a requested interaction, or checking reference overlap. Scale the work to the task; do not force a full audit or article workflow onto every edit.

## Specify before building

Write a compact implementation brief covering user goal, screen role, success criteria, content hierarchy, layout, typography, spacing, color roles, imagery, interactions, constraints and verification. Derive values from the chosen design system; avoid generic mandatory accent counts, typefaces, headline structures or wider uppercase labels. User constraints take precedence over examples from upstream sources.

During targeted refinement, preserve the approved system and change only the variables relevant to the feedback. Broad redesigns may need multiple coordinated changes; one-variable iteration is a diagnostic technique, not a universal rule.

## Study interactions with evidence

Inspect the live interaction and, when available and relevant, its supplied HTML/CSS/JS. Cover meaningful sections beyond the first viewport when analyzing a whole page. Associate each finding with the actual page, state and capture time.

For an interaction specification, record purpose, layout anatomy, initial state, trigger, transition, settled state, interruption behavior, keyboard/focus behavior, narrow-screen behavior and reduced-motion fallback. Choose implementation APIs after understanding the behavior and existing stack. A still frame cannot establish timing, scrolling or pointer behavior. Capture actual playback where needed; do not present a slideshow as evidence of website motion.

Do not inherit fixed inspiration counts, mandatory daily articles, automatic publishing or a required animation stack from a source workflow. Those are separate tasks, not part of a normal website build.

## Check originality proportionally

Compare the delivered design with the references actually used. Look for source-brand strings, copied proof claims, distinctive imagery, unusually matching compositions and signature motion sequences. Ordinary patterns alone are not proof of copying.

For a material overlap, identify both evidence locations, what matches, what differs, why it matters and a concrete adaptation that preserves the product goal. Recoloring alone does not fix copied identity. Do not declare legal infringement or certify originality from visual similarity. For requested comprehensive audits, inspect the relevant media, code and history with an appropriate audit workflow; report evidence gaps.

Record the implemented decisions and verification in the project's design system. Link to source references; retain only assets with appropriate rights.
